## 1. Who we are

Lurniq ("we", "us") is a student learning and study app operated by
`[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]`, `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`. Contact: `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`.

## 2. What data we collect (Phase 1 build)

- **Account data you provide:** display name, username, optional email, profile
  photo, bio, country/region, education level, school (optional), interests,
  learning goals, language/currency/timezone preferences.
- **Content you create:** posts, comments, study-group invitations, lesson
  photos, tasks, tutor reviews. Lesson/post images are captured with your
  device's camera or chosen from your photo library **only when you choose
  to**; the app requests permission at that moment and explains why.
- **What we do NOT collect:** precise location (study groups use places you
  type yourself — never share a home address), contacts, call logs, or
  payment card details (Phase 1 has no payments).

## 3. How we use data

To operate the app's features on your device: show your profile and posts to
other users per your privacy settings, organize your planner, and (with your
permission) send reminders. We do not sell personal data and do not use it
for third-party advertising.

## 4. Where data lives

Phase 1 stores your data **on your device only**. When cloud sync launches,
data will be stored with `[CLOUD PROVIDER / REGION]` under our data
processing terms, and this policy will be updated.

## 5. Your controls

- Per-field profile privacy (public / followers / private) in Edit profile.
- Block users to hide their content from you.
- **Delete your account** anytime in Settings → Delete account: this
  permanently removes your profile and content from the device (and our
  servers, once cloud sync exists).

## 6. Children and students

Lurniq is designed for students of all ages. Accounts for children under
`[AGE, e.g. 13]` require verifiable parental consent where the law requires
it. Messaging between adults and minors is restricted, and in-person
meetups are never required or arranged by the app.

## 7. Academic integrity

The community is for learning help and explanations, not for completing
graded assessments dishonestly. Content facilitating cheating may be removed.

## 8. Changes

We will update this policy as features launch (payments, messaging, AI
tools). Material changes will be announced in the app.

## 9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`.
